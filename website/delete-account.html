You can request deletion in the app under Progress → Account & data settings → Delete account. The web request form will be enabled only after the secure service and public contact are verified.

What an account deletion request does

The authenticated service removes the known SkinSnap account records and requests deletion of the matching Supabase sign-in account. The request does not accept a user ID from the browser. If a step fails, some records may already have been removed; where the account still exists, sign in again and retry.

Deletion cannot erase original photos in your gallery, app files on other devices, or data held by AI, hosting, authentication, payment, or backup providers under their own retention rules. In-app deletion attempts to remove account-scoped routine progress from the current device. Account deletion does not cancel a Google Play subscription; manage that separately in Google Play.

External deletion is disabled in this review copy. Use the in-app deletion option.

Before public release

The site owner must supply the verified public support/privacy contact, configure the public Supabase client and approved Worker origin, complete an end-to-end test with a disposable account, and approve publication. There is no unmonitored request queue or promised processing deadline.
