In-app deletion

  1. Open SkinSnap and go to Progress.
  2. Choose Account & data settings.
  3. Sign in if requested, then choose Delete account and data.
  4. Review the confirmation and confirm only if you understand the action is permanent.

The app sends an authenticated deletion request for the currently signed-in Supabase Auth account and attempts to remove account-scoped local routine progress. The live database's complete dependent-row deletion behavior must still be tested with a disposable account. Provider-side data deletion is not performed by the app.
