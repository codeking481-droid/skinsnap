Who can use SkinSnap

SkinSnap is for people aged 18 and older. The app asks users to confirm they are at least 18; it does not ask for a date of birth. The confirmation is stored with local app preferences, not as a server-side age record.

Information processed

When you create an account, Supabase Auth processes your email address, password, and account identifier. The password is sent to Supabase Auth for sign-in and is not saved by SkinSnap in local app storage. A display name may be saved if supplied. The app also processes routine completion, analysis history, offer and entitlement records, and optional routine preferences you choose to provide, such as skin feel, concerns, sensitivity, routine complexity, or budget.

The app does not intentionally use facial recognition or infer protected personal attributes. Its analysis is limited to visible cosmetic features and is not a medical diagnosis.

Photos, consent, and AI processing

Camera access is used to take a selected photo. The system photo picker lets you choose an image from your device. Before upload, the app explains the processing and asks for affirmative consent. It prepares a resized JPEG copy locally, then sends that copy over HTTPS to the authenticated SkinSnap API hosted on Cloudflare Workers.

The current Worker sends the image to Groq for Qwen-based visual observations. A separate Groq request receives validated text observations and optional routine preferences to generate general guidance; that routine request does not include the photo. Lighting, camera quality, filters, and image detail can affect results. Groq account settings and provider retention have not been verified by SkinSnap, so no provider deletion or retention guarantee is made.

The Worker processes the uploaded image in request memory and the inspected application code does not intentionally save raw photos to Supabase or an object-storage service. The app attempts to delete its prepared temporary JPEG when processing finishes or fails. App termination, device failure, or file-system errors may prevent cleanup. SkinSnap does not delete the original gallery image or guarantee removal of camera/picker cache copies. The selected image URI and current result may remain in app-session memory until replaced or cleared.

Saved account and device data

Supabase stores authentication and profile data, analysis job status and results, structured observations, generated routines, progress snapshots, and offer or entitlement records used by the app. Failed analyses can leave a failed-job/attempt record; the uploaded photo is not intentionally saved with it. No automatic expiration period for saved analysis results is configured in the inspected source.

Routine completion is also saved as a JSON file in the app's document storage, scoped to the account when signed in. Session tokens are stored with the platform secure-storage facility. Camera/gallery originals remain under the user's device or photo-provider control.

Service providers and international processing

  • Cloudflare: hosts the API and may process request and operational metadata. Workers observability is enabled; custom app logs include safe stage/correlation details, photo size and MIME type, but are not intended to include selfie bytes, credentials, or analysis text. Cloudflare's invocation metadata and retention settings have not been verified.
  • Supabase: provides authentication and the database. The project region, backup retention, and configured deletion constraints have not been re-verified.
  • Groq: receives the image for visual analysis and text observations/preferences for routine generation. Review Groq's current data controls; SkinSnap has not verified the account's applicable retention or zero-retention settings.
  • RevenueCat and Google Play: the app includes subscription code that may use these services when configured and purchases are available. They may process purchase, app-user, and entitlement identifiers. Payment-card information is handled by the store, not requested by SkinSnap. Current production configuration and vendor settings require owner verification.

These providers may process data in countries other than Nigeria. The exact project regions, contractual terms, and transfer safeguards require owner and legal review before publication.

Retention, security, and deletion

Analysis results have no configured automatic expiration in the inspected source. The app uses HTTPS for its service calls and platform secure storage for session tokens. Server secrets are held in Worker configuration, not in the mobile app. These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee complete security. Cloud and provider backup/log retention has not been verified.

You can request in-app account deletion from Progress → Account & data settings. The authenticated Worker removes the known account-scoped records and requests deletion of the Supabase Auth account. If a deletion step fails, earlier records may already be removed; sign in again and retry. The current live database schema has not been freshly verified, so completion of every historical or unlisted database record remains unconfirmed.

In-app deletion also attempts to remove account-scoped routine progress and the local sign-in. It cannot erase local files on your other devices, original gallery photos, or provider-held copies. Google Play subscription cancellation is separate. The public website deletion form is disabled in this review draft until approved configuration and live testing are complete.

Your choices and rights

Photo analysis is optional; you can decline consent, choose another photo, or use the app's non-photo features. Optional routine preferences can be left blank. Privacy rights depend on applicable law, including Nigerian data-protection requirements and the laws where the service is offered. Legal review and a verified public contact channel are required before requests can be publicly supported.

Changes

Material changes to the data processed, providers, purposes, or retention will require an updated policy and appropriate notice. This review draft has no effective date and is not a promise of legal compliance.

Retention upgrade ? draft disclosure

When enabled after the reviewed server and database update, My Skin Preferences stores your optional self-reported answers only after you choose to save them. This can include cosmetic goals, sensitivity, budget, region, products used, fragrance preference and experience. Relevant preferences are sent to Groq only with a consented analysis. User-edited routine names, instructions and schedules are stored separately from the original AI report. Diary dates, time zones, optional feelings, notes, cosmetic concerns, products and completion reports are saved to your secure profile. Routine edits and diary entries do not trigger AI calls or go to Groq. You may reset preferences, remove a custom routine, delete individual diary entries, or request account deletion. There is no configured automatic expiry for these records. Device routine checkmarks remain local. No diary photographs are stored. Guest access may be lost when app data is cleared or the app is reinstalled. This draft remains unpublished pending operator, contact and age-policy confirmation.
