Information processed

When you choose and consent to a photo analysis, SkinSnap receives the selected image through the app. The app prepares a temporary JPEG copy and sends it over HTTPS to the authenticated SkinSnap service. SkinSnap's current Worker sends the image inline to Groq for visible, non-diagnostic cosmetic observations. It then sends text observations and any optional, self-reported routine preferences to Groq to generate general skincare guidance.

The app does not intentionally store the raw image in its analysis database. It attempts to delete its prepared JPEG when request handling ends. The selected source image is not deleted by SkinSnap; camera/picker temporary files may remain subject to Android and app-cache behavior. An interrupted app may not complete temporary-file cleanup.

Structured analysis results, routines, account identifiers, and account/profile data may be stored in Supabase for history, routine presentation, and account operation. Routine-completion progress is stored in an app document on the device. Session tokens are stored using the platform secure store.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate your account, process the photo you selected, generate general cosmetic observations and routine guidance, display your saved analysis history, and operate account features. SkinSnap does not ask the AI service to identify you or infer protected personal traits.

Results are informational and can be affected by lighting, camera quality, and filters. SkinSnap is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional about medical concerns.

Service providers and international processing

SkinSnap uses Cloudflare Workers for its API, Supabase for authentication and structured data, Groq for image analysis and text-based routine generation. Provider-side logs, retention, security, and international processing are governed by their terms, account settings, and applicable agreements. SkinSnap cannot remove provider-held copies through this app.

Groq states that inference data is not retained by default but can be retained temporarily for reliability or abuse monitoring; project settings have not been confirmed.

Retention and deletion

SkinSnap does not configure an expiration period for saved analysis results in the inspected application. The app offers authenticated account deletion under Progress → Account & data settings. The live database’s full deletion-cascade behavior and provider-side deletion are not yet verified. Do not treat a successful app response as proof that every provider copy was deleted.

An external account-deletion request channel is not configured. A verified public contact and working request process are required before this page is published.

Security and choices

App API requests use HTTPS. Session tokens are stored in the device secure store. You may decline photo consent, select another photo, or leave without requesting analysis. Consent applies to the selected photo and current in-memory app session.
