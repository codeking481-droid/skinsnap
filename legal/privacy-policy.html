Privacy Policy for SkinSnap

Last updated: October 30, 2026
Package: com.skinsnap.app
Contact: alphatekxcompany@gmail.com

1. Overview

SkinSnap is a cosmetic skin appearance tracking app. We do not provide medical diagnosis. The app analyzes user-uploaded photos to provide cosmetic observations like hydration appearance, texture, evenness, and blemish appearance.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account: Email, user ID via Supabase Auth.
  • Photos: Front and side profile photos you choose to upload for analysis. Photos are processed for cosmetic analysis only.
  • Analysis Results: Cosmetic observations, image quality assessment, routine preferences.
  • App Activity: Routine completion, diary entries, streaks, stored locally and in Supabase if signed in.
  • Purchases: Subscription status via RevenueCat and Google Play Billing. We do not collect card numbers.
  • Technical: Device type, OS, app version, crash logs via Cloudflare Worker logs.

3. How We Process Photos

Photos are sent to our Cloudflare Worker, which forwards them to Groq AI for cosmetic analysis. Groq processes the image to return cosmetic observations only. We do not use photos for biometric identification. Photos are retained only as long as needed to generate and save your result in your history if you are signed in. You can delete individual results or your account at any time.

4. Third Parties

  • Supabase: Auth and database storage.
  • Cloudflare Workers: API gateway and log handling.
  • Groq: AI cosmetic analysis provider.
  • RevenueCat & Google Play Billing: Subscription verification.
  • Expo / EAS: Build and update infrastructure.

5. Data Safety Declaration Alignment

We declare in Google Play Data Safety: Photos collected for app functionality, not shared; Account info for functionality; Purchases for functionality. No data sold. Encryption in transit. You can request deletion.

6. Retention

Account and history retained until account deletion. Photos associated with saved results retained until result deleted. Logs retained for short-term debugging (max 30 days) in Cloudflare.

7. Your Rights

You can: access history in app, delete individual diary entries/results, delete account via in-app flow or via our deletion page. For data requests, email alphatekxcompany@gmail.com.

8. Children

SkinSnap is 18+ only. We block under-18 via age gate. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Changes

We will update this page and notify in app if material changes occur.
