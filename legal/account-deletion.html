Option 1: Delete in App (Recommended)

1. Open SkinSnap app
2. Sign in
3. Go to Settings / Profile
4. Tap "Delete Account"
5. Confirm deletion

This deletes: Supabase auth account, saved analysis history, diary entries, routine progress, custom routines linked to account. Local app cache cleared on next launch.

What is retained: Anonymous purchase receipts for Google Play compliance (no personal photo retained), short-term Worker logs (max 30 days) then auto-deleted.

Option 2: Request via Email

If you cannot access app, email alphatekxcompany@gmail.com from your account email with subject "Delete my SkinSnap account - com.skinsnap.app". Include your account email.

We will delete within 7 business days and confirm via email. You will receive confirmation that data listed above has been deleted.

Data Deletion Details

  • Photos in saved results deleted when result deleted or account deleted.
  • Analysis results deleted with account.
  • Diary and routine data deleted with account.
  • RevenueCat/Google Play purchase history: we keep entitlement status only for fraud prevention, no card data.
