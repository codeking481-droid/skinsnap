# Google Play store listing review

**Prepared:** 23 October 2026
**Status:** Draft; requires owner and legal review and an approved public Privacy Policy URL.

## Suggested product description

> SkinSnap is an 18+ skincare companion for general cosmetic observations and practical routine guidance. With your consent, a selected photo is securely sent to SkinSnap and Groq for visual observations; text observations and optional preferences are used to prepare routine suggestions. Lighting, camera quality, and filters can affect results. SkinSnap is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent medical conditions. Consult a qualified healthcare professional about medical concerns.

Do not use this copy until the live provider configuration and the user-facing policy are verified. It does not claim clinical accuracy, provider non-retention, or deletion of copies outside SkinSnap's control.

## Claims to avoid

- Medical-grade, clinically accurate, disease detection, diagnosis, treatment, cure, or prevention.
- Guaranteed improvements or outcome timelines.
- Beauty scores, attractiveness percentages/rankings, or shaming language.
- “Your photos are never stored or shared,” “deleted everywhere,” or “AI providers never retain data.”
- A subscription price, discount, free trial, or purchase-success claim not shown and confirmed by the configured store.
