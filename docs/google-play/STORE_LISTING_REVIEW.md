# Google Play store listing review

**Prepared:** 2026-10-22  
**Status:** Draft; final listing requires owner and legal review.

## Safe positioning

Suggested concise description:

> SkinSnap helps adults build a consistent skincare routine with general cosmetic observations of visible skin characteristics. With your consent, a selected photo is processed by SkinSnap and Google Gemini; text observations are sent to Groq to generate general routine guidance. Results can be affected by lighting, camera quality, and filters. SkinSnap is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional about medical concerns.

The operator must confirm age eligibility/target audience and Google Gemini API terms before using this copy. Google Gemini API terms say its service is not to be used for API clients directed to or likely accessed by under-18s; a self-attestation alone may not satisfy that restriction.

## Claims to avoid

- Medical-grade, clinically accurate, disease detection, acne diagnosis, cure, treatment, prevention.
- Guaranteed improvements or outcome timelines.
- Beauty scores, attractiveness percentages/rankings, shaming language.
- "Your data/photos are never stored or shared."
- "Deleted everywhere" or "AI providers never retain data."
- Subscription price, discount, or purchase-success claims not returned by the configured store.

## Listing checks before submission

- [ ] State supported Android devices and camera requirements.
- [ ] Add the non-medical disclaimer and professional-care guidance.
- [ ] Link the active public Privacy Policy.
- [ ] Describe photo processing and deletion honestly.
- [ ] Align the Data safety form and health declaration with actual release behavior and third-party SDKs.
- [ ] Verify screenshots contain no fabricated AI outputs or personal data.
- [ ] Confirm target audience and age restrictions against Gemini API terms.
- [ ] Remove prototype/demo language only after relevant live services and policies are truly ready.
